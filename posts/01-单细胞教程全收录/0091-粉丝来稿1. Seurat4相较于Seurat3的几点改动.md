2.FindNeighbors

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJTZ0PAotm6HriciaNkJfsbicJA31YqibhSZOAIfxNibE0blibyPGNkLiadxwzMg/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJTEibOfQmUibFo1TWGFrZ8LI3j3ZN9qAx7HgOkrkI8sgwEbqpVH4MBDESg/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)在“annoy”中设置了用于识别k-nearest neighbors的默认方法。这种方法广泛应用于许多高维分析，包括单细胞分析。广泛测试证实“annoy”大大提高速度且降低对内存的需求，对下游结果的影响可忽略不计。利用nn.method="rann"，可切换回以前默认模式。![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJT9Zzd1lKB0SwRxGa2K2TFUMTLxa4y2FeHzUy5ARvwyD3mVwFloGqhww/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJT4fWv4qtNOib05eia9YjiaabjHRtwFYQOf0ed7ZbssruN898rzEHiciaFN3g/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)

3. FindMarkers

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJTZ0PAotm6HriciaNkJfsbicJA31YqibhSZOAIfxNibE0blibyPGNkLiadxwzMg/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJTEibOfQmUibFo1TWGFrZ8LI3j3ZN9qAx7HgOkrkI8sgwEbqpVH4MBDESg/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)重构了FindMarkers()函数代码。现在默认预过滤基因和报告fold change使用底数为2，替代了之前使用natural log。即默认状态下FindMarkers() 将用avg_log2FC，取代avg_logFC。可以利用base = exp(1)进行更改。![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJT9Zzd1lKB0SwRxGa2K2TFUMTLxa4y2FeHzUy5ARvwyD3mVwFloGqhww/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/Ljib4So7yuWiadRtBxBtRH1nZ4ZpFgkFJT4fWv4qtNOib05eia9YjiaabjHRtwFYQOf0ed7ZbssruN898rzEHiciaFN3g/640?wx_fmt=png&wxfrom=5&wx_lazy=1&wx_co=1)
