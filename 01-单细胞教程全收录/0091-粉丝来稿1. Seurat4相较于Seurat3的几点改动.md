1.删除的函数

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)

a.GeneActivity代替CreateGeneActivityMatrix

b.RunTFIDF和RunSVD代替RunLSI

c.ReadAlevin和ReadAlevinCsv移至SeuratWrappers

d.ExportToCellbrowser和StopCellbrowser移至SeuratWrappers

![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)![](../../images/399758fa7d6941c2.png)

2.FindNeighbors

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)在“annoy”中设置了用于识别k-nearest neighbors的默认方法。这种方法广泛应用于许多高维分析，包括单细胞分析。广泛测试证实“annoy”大大提高速度且降低对内存的需求，对下游结果的影响可忽略不计。利用nn.method="rann"，可切换回以前默认模式。![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)![](../../images/399758fa7d6941c2.png)

3. FindMarkers

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)重构了FindMarkers()函数代码。现在默认预过滤基因和报告fold change使用底数为2，替代了之前使用natural log。即默认状态下FindMarkers() 将用avg_log2FC，取代avg_logFC。可以利用base = exp(1)进行更改。![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)![](../../images/399758fa7d6941c2.png)

4.IntegrateData/TransferData

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)对锚定点权重矩阵函数的计算进行了细微的更改，但不会对下游分析产生有意义的差异。![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)![](../../images/399758fa7d6941c2.png)

5. SCTransform

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)在SCTransform()中修改了部分默认参数。在预估mu和theta间正则化关系时，ncells参数设置为5000\. vst() 函数对正则化过程进行了细微更改以改善速度和内存。![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)![](../../images/399758fa7d6941c2.png)

6. Integrative multimodal analysis

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)新增WNN分析，用于学习每个细胞中每种模态的信息内容，并根据两种模态的加权组合来定义细胞状态。适用于：CITE-seq, ASAP-seq, 10X Genomics ATAC + RNA, and SHARE-seq.![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)![](../../images/399758fa7d6941c2.png)

7. Rapid mapping of query datasets to references

![](../../images/fbfc4b8be383f432.png)![](../../images/007e970626afa0b7.png)可以将任何人类PBMC单细胞数据映射到我们的参考数据集上，从而自动执行可视化，聚类注释和差异表达过程。![](../../images/7d247e6c6ef19f87.png)
